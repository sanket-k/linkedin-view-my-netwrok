How do I get my Connections.csv? Visual Guide (3 steps)

LinkedIn lets you export all your connections for free. It takes about 1 minute to request:

Step 1: Request your data

On LinkedIn's download page, select Download larger data archive (or choose Connections) and click Request archive.

Step 2: Download the ZIP

LinkedIn prepares your archive and sends an email notification (usually 10–30 mins). Revisit the page and click Download archive.

Step 3: Find the CSV and upload it

Unzip the downloaded folder on your computer. Look inside and drag only Connections.csv into the app.

How to get your LinkedIn Connections.csv

3 annotated screenshots • from LinkedIn to Connections Explorer

Open LinkedIn Settings ↗
STEP 1 / 3: Request your data — Start from the direct LinkedIn data-download link, select larger data archive (or Connections), and click Request archive.
